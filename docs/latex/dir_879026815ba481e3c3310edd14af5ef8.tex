\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
For WPS Push Button method,after the ESP32 boots up and prints that WPS has started, press the button that looks something like \href{https://www.verizon.com/supportresources/images/fqgrouter-frontview-wps-button.png}{\texttt{ this}} on your router. In case you dont find anything similar, check your router specs if it does really support WPS Push functionality.

As for WPS Pin Mode, it will output a 8 digit Pin on the Serial Monitor that will change every 2 minutes if it hasn\textquotesingle{}t connected. You need to log in to your router (generally reaching 192.\+168.\+0.\+1) and enter the pin shown in Serial Monitor in the WPS Settings of your router.\hypertarget{README.md_autotoc_md697}{}\doxysubsubsubsection{\texorpdfstring{WPS Push Button Failure}{WPS Push Button Failure}}\label{README.md_autotoc_md697}
